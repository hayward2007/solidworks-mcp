# Gear generators

## What they cover
- `gear_design` (no CAD): spur/helical pairs, internal pairs, racks, bevel pairs, planetary sets, worm sets. Diameters, profile shifts, centre distance, contact and overlap ratio, tip clearances, interference warnings, mesh phases, and the basis of every default.
- `create_gear` (native SolidWorks features): spur, helical, herringbone, internal (ring) gear, rack (spur or helical), straight bevel, spiral bevel, ZA worm, worm wheel, worm set. `build_mate: true` also builds the mating gear (opposite hand for external pairs, same hand for internal) and rolls the pair through one pitch with temporary bodies.
- Covers the gear types of the OpenSCAD library chrisspen/gears (except herringbone bevel gears and racks with screw holes; planetary sets are computed by gear_design and built part by part), implemented from the standards (ISO 21771, ISO 53, Gleason/AGMA 2005, DIN 3975). That library is CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, so none of its code is used. Cycloidal and strain-wave drives are not generated (buy as units, see gears).

## Geometry, not drawings
- Cylindrical tooth spaces are generated by the ISO 53 counterpart rack (law of gearing): exact involute, trochoidal root fillet, undercut where it really occurs. Flank points match the involute to 1e-9 mm; spline through-points stay within about 5 um up to module 10.
- Helical teeth: cut sweep with constant twist along the gear axis (right hand turns counter-clockwise along +Z). Herringbone: helical half mirrored about its end face.
- Internal gears: involute flanks with a circular root fillet (approximates the shaper-cut root) [heur]; tips shortened to the base circle when z is too small (warning).
- Bevel: Tredgold back-cone sections (converging depth, apex-centred cones), loft cut with 4 guide curves; spiral teeth follow a face-mill circular arc (nominal cutter radius >= R_m) [cat]. Cutter edge radius 0.38 m (ISO 53 A), reduced to 0.30 m or less automatically when the root land would close.
- Worm wheel: involute helical gear with helix = worm lead angle, same hand, plus a toroidal throat [heur: point contact; for hobbed line contact use the gear maker's data].

## Frames (for mates and placement)
- Cylindrical gears: axis +Z through the origin, z = 0 face on the Front plane, a tooth space centred on +X at z = 0. A reference axis "Gear axis" is created.
- External pair: pinion at the origin, gear 2 centre at (+a, 0). Internal pair: ring at the origin, pinion centre at (+a, 0). Rotate each part about its own Z axis by `mesh.phase_deg`.
- Rack: datum (pitch) line on X, teeth toward +Y, starts and ends at a tooth-space centre. Pinion above at (k p, r + x m) rotated by -90 - 180/z deg; rack and pinion need opposite hands.
- Bevel: apex at the origin, axis +Z, gear body at +Z. 90 deg pair: gear 2 axis along +X with coincident apexes, rotated by `mesh.phase_deg` about its own axis.
- Worm: axis +Z from z = 0 to the length. Wheel: as a helical gear; worm axis 90 deg to the wheel axis at the centre distance, mid-plane of the wheel.

## Defaults and their basis
| Item | Default | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Pressure angle, profile | 20 deg, ISO 53 A (h_a 1, h_f 1.25, rho_f 0.38 m) | [std] |
| Backlash (pair, circumferential) | 0.04 m, each gear thinned by half | [heur] machined 0.03-0.08 mm at m 1 |
| Face width | 10 m (cylindrical), min(R_e/3, 10 m) bevel, 0.75 d_a1 worm wheel | [heur] KHK / Shigley 3p-5p, AGMA, DIN 3975 |
| Profile shift | 0; small pinions get x_min (no undercut), gear -x (V-zero, same centre distance) | [std] ISO 21771 / DIN 3960 |
| Given centre distance | shift sum from inv(alpha_w); pinion keeps x_min, rest split equally; tip shortening for clearance | [calc] ISO 21771 |
| Internal gear rim | d_f + 6 m | [heur] ISO 6336-3 backup ratio >= 1.2 |
| Bevel proportions | Gleason (working depth 2.0 m straight, 1.7 m spiral; balanced thickness) | [std] ANSI/AGMA 2005 |
| Worm | ZA, q = 10, h_a = m, clearance 0.2 m | [std] DIN 3975, q [heur] |
| Rack height | datum 9 m above the back | [cat] KHK SRS |

## Self-checks in every create_gear result
- `volume_error_pct`: SolidWorks volume vs exact section area x face width (cylindrical, racks, worms); normally |error| < 0.1 %. Larger values mean a broken sketch or cut.
- `bodies` 1, `errors` empty, `box_mm` as expected; helical builds probe the material to confirm the hand.
- `mesh_check.max_interference_mm3` (with build_mate): 0 for cylindrical and rack pairs with backlash; bevel (Tredgold approximation) about 0.03-0.1 mm3; same-hand helical or spiral pairs show 50-160 mm3 (wrong hands).
- Warnings from the gear calculation: undercut, pointed or thin tips (< 0.25 m), contact ratio < 1.2, small tip clearance, internal z2 - z1 < 10, involute interference.

## Typical calls
```json
{"type": "spur", "module": 1.5, "teeth": 18, "mate_teeth": 36, "build_mate": true, "bore": 8, "keyway": true}
{"type": "helical", "module": 2, "teeth": 20, "helix_angle": 20, "hand": "right", "face_width": 15}
{"type": "internal", "module": 1, "teeth": 72, "face_width": 8}
{"type": "spiral_bevel", "module": 2, "teeth": 16, "mate_teeth": 24, "build_mate": true, "bore": 8}
{"type": "worm_set", "module": 1.5, "starts": 2, "teeth": 30}
```
Planetary: `gear_design {"kind": "planetary", "ratio": 5, "planets": 3, "module": 1}` gives (sun, planet, ring) and the rotation of each part; build sun and planet with `type: spur`, the ring with `type: internal`.

## Assembly placement
1. Insert the parts, fix gear 1 (origin coincident).
2. Gear 2: axis concentric with a reference axis at the centre distance (or coincident apexes for bevels), faces flush.
3. Rotate gear 2 by `phase_deg` about its own axis (temporary angle mate, then delete it), then add the Gear mate z1:z2. A gear mate locks the phase that exists when it is created.
4. check_interference: 0 (with backlash).
